02 / under the leaves

A voice made of moving air.

Physical, not sampled

Chimera models lungs, vocal folds, a vocal tract, lips, and a nose. The audio here is a real render of that airway, not a recording of a person.

Three ways to sing

Female, male, and child are three built-in body presets. Switching voices loads a different render of the same score; it does not change the melody.

A sandbox, deliberately small

This page uses prerecorded WAV masters and lightweight MP3 previews. The singing head is a lightweight canvas animation driven by the note timings, not the full Rust/WebAssembly synth.

What I made and what I verified

The lyrics are by Amber. I composed an original 28-note melody in D major at 96 BPM, with four phrases and a rising three-note “blue.” The audio was generated with Chimera commit eed4a5ff2695ad151df2210d012b2a4ab584f099; all three voice files were checked for nonzero, unclipped output and matching 19.45-second duration.

The page is a listening sandbox, not a voice-cloning tool. The interactive synthesizer and its full editor live in the linked Chimera repository. No external audio, vocal likeness, or accompaniment was used.
